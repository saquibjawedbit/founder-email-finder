How to find the founder's email of a company from its website

Look on the company's own about, team and contact pages for the person called founder, owner or CEO. If the site shows their email, use it. If not, use the format the site's other emails follow (for example jane@ or jane.smith@); if there are none, first name @ the company domain is the most common small-company format. Treat that as a guess, not a fact.

How to do it

By hand, for one company

  1. Open the about, team and contact pages. Note who is called founder, co-founder, owner or CEO.
  2. Search the pages for an email with a person's name in it. That shows you the format.
  3. Build the founder's address in the same format. If you only find info@ or hello@, try firstname@domain.
