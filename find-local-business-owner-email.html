How to find the owner email of a local business (plumbers, contractors)

Local businesses like plumbers and contractors rarely publish the owner's personal email, but most publish a service inbox and many name the owner on the home or about page ("family-owned by…"). Use the owner's name with the service inbox, or first name @ their domain as a labelled guess.

How to do it

  1. Collect the websites (from a directory, a spreadsheet or a Google Maps scrape).
  2. Paste them into the tool. Each row gives the owner if the site names one, an owner email guess, the best company inbox and the phone.
  3. Filter on ownerFound to keep only the businesses where an owner was named.

Questions

Do plumbers and contractors publish their email?

Most publish a shared service or sales inbox. Few publish the owner's personal address.

Is a guessed owner email reliable?

In my tests, founder email guesses were exactly right for 70% of 189 real people, and 76% when the site shows its email format. Guesses are labelled and never checked by sending mail.
