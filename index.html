How it works

  1. Give it companies. Paste websites or domains, give a Google Maps dataset ID, or pick a Singapore industry from the official ACRA register.
  2. It reads each company's own site. Home, contact, about and team pages, for the people it names and the addresses it shows.
  3. US dentists and clinics: it also checks the official NPI registry. The free federal list of healthcare providers often names the practice owner. On 20 US dental practices this named the owner for 8 and a dentist at the practice for 10 more. How that works.
  4. You get one row per company. Download it as a spreadsheet, CSV or JSON.

Questions

What do I get for each company?

One row with the founder or owner's name and email (labelled published on site, guessed from site email format, or guessed common format), the best company email, phone numbers, LinkedIn and other social profiles, and the website's tech stack.

Where do the emails come from?

Only from each company's own website: home, contact, about and team pages. There is no database of emails collected elsewhere.

How accurate are the founder emails?

In my tests, founder email guesses were exactly right for 70% of 189 real people, and 76% when the site shows its email format. Guesses are never checked by sending mail.

What does it cost?

$1.50 per 1,000 companies for websites or a Google Maps dataset, and $5 per 1,000 for the Singapore industry search. Rows with nothing found are free. Apify gives $5 of free credit every month.

How does it compare with other contact scrapers?

On 63 small-business websites, against the most-used contact scraper on Apify: emails for 57 vs 51 sites, phones for 54 vs 40, emails from other organisations 5 vs 22, and 81 seconds vs 416 seconds.

Can I remove my details?

Yes. Open a removal request and I will remove them.

Results are public business contact details taken from each company's own website, from the official company register for the Singapore search, or from the US NPI registry for US healthcare practices. Guessed emails are always labelled as guessed and are never checked by sending mail. If your details appear in a result and you want them removed, open a removal request and I will remove them.
