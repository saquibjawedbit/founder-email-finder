How to find a marketing agency owner's email

Small marketing agencies almost always name their founder on the about page, and many use first@ addresses. Take the founder's name from the about page and either use the email the site shows or write to firstname@agency-domain as a labelled guess. For a list of agencies, my tool does this per website.

How to do it

  1. Build a list of agency websites (a directory, Clutch, a Google Maps search for "marketing agency").
  2. Paste the websites into the tool.
  3. Use rows marked published on site as is; treat guessed rows as likely, not certain.
