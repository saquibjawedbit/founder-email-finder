How to find a dentist practice owner's email

Most dental practices publish one shared inbox and name their dentists on a "Meet the doctor" page, often without saying who owns the practice. For US practices, the free federal NPI registry fills much of that gap: on 20 real US practices my tool named the owner for 8 and a dentist working at the practice for 10 more. Write to the practice inbox and address that person by name.

How the NPI registry works

  • Nearly every US dentist, doctor and practice that bills insurance has an NPI number, and the government publishes the list free at npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov.
  • A practice that is a company has its own record, which names an official and their title. When the title says owner, president or partner, and the record's name and address match the practice, the tool uses that person as the owner.
  • Many small practices have no company record: the dentist bills under their own number. Then the tool looks for individual dentists whose registered practice address is the practice's street address. When the site names its dentists, only those names count.
  • Several dentists at one address and no word from the site on who owns it: all are listed as "dentist at practice", none as owner. Unclear matches are left empty, not guessed.

What is official and what is guessed

  • Official: the person's name, their NPI number, their registered title and practice address. Each row links to the registry page.
  • Guessed: the owner's email. It is built from the practice's email format, like first@practice.com, and labelled guessed. It is never checked by sending mail. Practices that only show a Gmail inbox get no guess.
  • Not claimed: that a "dentist at practice" owns it. Use their name in the greeting, and send to the practice inbox.

How to do it

  1. Scrape dentists in your area from Google Maps, or collect their websites.
  2. Run the list through the tool. US practices are looked up in the NPI registry automatically, at no extra cost.
  3. Write to the practice inbox, greeting the owner or the dentist by name.

Questions

Do dentist websites list the owner's email?

Rarely. They usually list one practice inbox and name the dentist on a separate "Meet the doctor" page, often without saying who owns the practice.

Can the tool find the owner dentist's name?

For US practices, partly. On 20 US dental practices it named the owner for 8 (6 from the federal NPI registry, 2 from the practice's own site). For 10 more it named the dentist registered at the practice's address, labelled "dentist at practice", because the registry does not say who owns those practices. 2 got no name.

Is the NPI registry data official?

Yes. The NPI registry is the US government's public list of healthcare providers, free to search. The name, title and practice address come straight from it. The owner email is still a guess built from the practice's email format, and is labelled as one.

Does this work outside the US?

The registry lookup is US only. Elsewhere you get the practice inbox, phone, and any owner the site itself names.

What does it cost?
