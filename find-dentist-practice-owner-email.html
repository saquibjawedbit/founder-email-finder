How to find a dentist practice owner's email

Most dental practices publish one shared inbox (info@ or the front desk) and name the owner on a "Meet the dentist" page as "Dr. First Last, owner". Write to the shared inbox and address the owner by name; reception forwards it. In bulk, my tool gets the inbox and phone reliably, but in my test it did not pick up the owner's name from dentist sites.

How to do it

  1. Run your list of practice websites (or a Google Maps dataset of dentists) through the tool to get each practice's email and phone.
  2. For the practices you care about most, open the Meet the dentist page and note who is called owner or principal dentist.
  3. Email the shared inbox with the owner's name in the first line.

Questions

Do dentist websites list the owner's email?

Rarely. They usually list one practice inbox and name the owner dentist on a separate page.

Can the tool find the owner dentist's name?

Not reliably yet: on 5 dentist sites I tested it found none of the owners. It did find the practice phone on all 5 and the practice email on 3.
