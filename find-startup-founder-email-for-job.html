How to find a startup founder's email to ask for a job

At a small startup, the founder often reads job emails personally. Find the founder's name on the company's about page, then write to firstname@company-domain, the most common format at small companies. Keep it short, say what you would do for them, and send one follow-up at most.

How to do it

  1. Make a list of startups you would like to work for.
  2. Paste their websites into the tool. You get each founder's name and an email marked published or guessed.
  3. If it is a guess, also try the other likely addresses the tool lists, one at a time.
  4. Write three to five sentences: who you are, one thing you would build or fix for them, a link to your work.

Questions

Is it OK to email a founder about a job?

Yes, at a small company that is normal. Keep it short and specific and do not send more than one follow-up.

What if the email is guessed?

It may bounce. The tool lists other likely addresses; try them one at a time. In my tests, founder email guesses were exactly right for 70% of 189 real people, and 76% when the site shows its email format.
