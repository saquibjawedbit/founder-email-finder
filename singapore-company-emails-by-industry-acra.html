How to find emails of Singapore companies by industry (ACRA)

Start from ACRA, Singapore's official company register (published on data.gov.sg), filter by industry code (SSIC), find each company's own website, and read it for contacts. My tool does all of that in one run: pick an industry, get one row per company with its UEN, registration date, verified website, email and phone. It costs $5 per 1,000 companies, and companies with no contacts are free.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool and leave Websites empty.
  2. Under Find companies by industry, choose Singapore and add industry codes, for example 69201 accounting, 70205 marketing and PR consultancy, 62011 software development.
  3. Optional: a postal code start (079), minimum years operating, maximum companies. Press Start.

A website is accepted only when it shows the company's UEN or registered name, or its brand plus a sign it is in Singapore. Codes work better than keywords: most companies leave their own activity description empty in the register. An optional web search finds sites traded under another brand; it runs Apify's Google search tool at about $0.0045 per company searched, billed on top.

Questions

What is ACRA?

The Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority, Singapore's company register. Its company list is published as open data on data.gov.sg.

Does ACRA publish company emails?

No. The register has the name, UEN, industry code, address and status. The emails and phones come from each company's own website.

What does the Singapore search cost?

$5 per 1,000 companies with contacts; companies without contacts are free. The optional web search costs about $0.0045 per company searched.
