How to guess a company's email format (first@, first.last@) and how accurate it is

Find two or three staff emails on the company's own site and copy their pattern: first@, first.last@, flast@ (first initial plus last name) and so on. If the site shows no personal emails, first@ is the most common at small companies. Measured on 189 real people, my tool's founder guesses were exactly right 70% of the time, and 76% when the site showed its email format.

first_last@ / last@jane_smith@acme.com / smith@acme.com

How to do it

  1. Look on the team, staff and contact pages for addresses with names in them.
  2. Match each address to the person's name to see the pattern.
  3. Apply the pattern to the person you want. Use the guess as a guess: it is right most of the time, not always.

The tool does this for you and labels each founder email published on site, guessed from site email format or guessed (common format), with other likely addresses.
