Free bulk founder email finder: how many lookups $5 of free Apify credit covers

About 3,333 companies a month. Apify gives every account $5 of free credit each month, and my tool costs $1.50 per 1,000 companies for pasted websites or a Google Maps dataset, so $5 covers about 3,333 companies. The Singapore industry search costs $5 per 1,000, so $5 covers about 1,000. Rows where nothing was found are not charged, so the real number is usually higher.

What my own test runs would have cost you

For the examples on this site I ran 39 real websites. 36 returned contacts and 3 did not (sites that blocked automated visits), so a user would pay for 36: $0.054. The Singapore example returned 6 companies, 3 with contacts: $0.015.

How to use the free credit

  1. Open the tool on Apify and sign in (free account, $5 of free credit every month).
  2. Paste websites, one per line, or give a Google Maps dataset ID, or pick a Singapore industry.
  3. Press Start. You get one row per company; download it as a spreadsheet, CSV or JSON.
