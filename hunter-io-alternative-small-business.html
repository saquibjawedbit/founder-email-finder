Hunter.io alternative for small businesses

If you look up small businesses now and then, a pay-per-use tool that reads each company's own website is cheaper than a monthly plan. Mine costs $1.50 per 1,000 companies, rows with nothing found are free, and Apify's $5 monthly free credit covers about 3,333 companies. Hunter.io costs $49 a month for 2,000 credits, with 50 free credits a month (Hunter's pricing as reported in Oct–Nov 2026).

What is different

  • Where emails come from. Hunter has a database of emails collected across the web and can verify them. My tool reads only the company's own website (home, contact, about and team pages). It has no database and never sends mail to check a guess.
  • What you pay for. Hunter is a subscription. Mine charges only for companies where it found an email, phone or social profile.
  • What you get per row. Founder or owner name and email (labelled published or guessed), the best company inbox, phones, LinkedIn and other profiles, and the site's tech stack.

Questions

Is this cheaper than Hunter.io?

For occasional use, yes: $1.50 per 1,000 companies with no subscription, against $49 a month for 2,000 Hunter credits. Hunter adds a verified email database, which this tool does not have.

Does it verify emails?

It drops emails whose domain cannot receive mail. It never sends mail to check a guessed address; guesses are labelled.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You pay per company on Apify, and Apify gives $5 of free credit every month.
